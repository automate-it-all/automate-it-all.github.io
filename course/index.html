The Twelve Lessons

  1. Bare VPS to a Public HTTPS Page — a stranger loads your page over HTTPS; root SSH login is off and a firewall is up. Free.
  2. Caddy as Your Web Server — your site serves compressed files with security headers from a Caddyfile you wrote. Free.
  3. systemd Runs Your Own Service — your app answers behind Caddy and comes back by itself after a reboot. Free.
  4. Tailscale: One Private Network — your laptop reaches the VPS over a tailnet address with no open port.
  5. Tailscale Funnel: Publish Without a Port — one service is public through the funnel while the firewall stays shut.
  6. Docker Compose on the Box — a container stack starts, stops and survives a reboot as one unit.
  7. Caddy Reverse Proxy for Many Services — three services answer on three subdomains from one Caddyfile.
  8. Hermes: An Agent That Lives on Your Box — a Hermes agent runs as a systemd unit and logs to the journal.
  9. The Telegram Gateway — you send a message from your phone and your box answers.
  10. One Bot Token per Box — two boxes answer two chats and neither can read the other's.
  11. Backups That Actually Restore — a restic snapshot goes to a remote and you restore it into an empty directory.
  12. Auto-Shutdown and Monitoring — the box sleeps when idle, wakes on demand, and tells you when it does.
