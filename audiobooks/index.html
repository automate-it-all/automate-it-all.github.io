Audiobooks Player

A browser-only audiobook reader for public-domain EPUBs, read aloud in English or Spanish. It fetches each book straight from Standard Ebooks or Wikisource, reads it with an on-device Piper voice or the OS voice, and tracks your place per book: no book text ships in the repo, and no server is required.
